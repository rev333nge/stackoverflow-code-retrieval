STACKOVERFLOW CODE SEARCH — RESULTS
===================================

BM25 BASELINE  (Phase 1)  —  variant A, TEST set, 19,065 queries
    NDCG@10      0.6893
    MRR@10       0.7190
    Recall@100   0.9309

    This is the line every later phase must beat.


WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN
    NDCG@10 0.69     ranking quality in the top 10 (1.0 = perfect). Relevant
                     answers land near the top, but often not exactly at #1.
    MRR@10  0.72     the first useful answer sits around rank ~1.4 on average.
    Recall@100 0.93  93% of relevant answers appear somewhere in the top 100.


KEY READ — retrieval is strong, RANKING is the gap
    Recall@100 is high (0.93) but NDCG@10 is lower (0.69): BM25 almost always
    FINDS the right answers, it just doesn't always rank them at the top.
        -> ranking has the most room       -> Phase 6 (learning-to-rank)
        -> the ~7% never retrieved are
           synonym / typo misses           -> Phase 3 (dense retrieval)
