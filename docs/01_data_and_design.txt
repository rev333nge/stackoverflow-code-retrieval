STACKOVERFLOW CODE SEARCH — DATA & DESIGN
=========================================

GOAL
  Type a plain-English question, search a pool of Stack Overflow answers, and
  rank the best answers first. Built in phases: each phase adds one technique
  and is measured against the previous one. This file covers the Phase 1 setup.


DATASET
  Source : official Stack Exchange Data Dump, via ClickHouse's public parquet
           mirror (posts table, 2008 - Mar 2024).
  Slice  : questions tagged 'pandas' or 'numpy' — one coherent domain.
  Why    : a focused domain makes the metrics meaningful and gives the later
           code-aware tokenizer (Phase 2) real material to work on.


FILTERING  (principle: filter the LABELS, not the DATA)
  Keep every pandas/numpy question and ALL of its answers — weak answers stay
  on purpose, as distractors the ranker must push down.
  A question counts as an EVAL QUERY (trustworthy ground truth) only if:
    - it has an accepted answer that exists in the dump
    - its score >= 1
    - it is not closed


FINAL COUNTS
  documents (answers) . . . 537,134
  questions . . . . . . . . 379,088
  eval queries  . . . . . . 137,131


DOCUMENT = one answer. Two ways to build its searchable text were tested:
    Variant A : question title + answer body                  (chosen)
    Variant B : question title + question body + answer body
  A won (see 02_results.txt). The question body just added length and noise.


RELEVANCE GRADES (qrels) — from Stack Overflow's own signals, not our opinion
    3  accepted answer        (asker marked it as the solution)
    2  answer score >= 4      (community endorsed)
    1  answer score 1 - 3
    0  answer score <= 0      (ignored / downvoted)
  245,287 (query, answer) judgments over the 137,131 eval queries.


TRAIN / VAL / TEST — split by TIME, not randomly
    train   2008 - 2020    102,143    tune / train models
    val     2021            15,923    pick options (e.g. variant A vs B)
    test    2022 - 2024     19,065    final score, touched once
  Splitting by date stops near-duplicate questions leaking across the sets and
  mirrors serving questions asked in the future.


BM25 SETTINGS (Phase 1 baseline)
    library    bm25s
    tokenizer  Phase 1: lowercase + split on non-alphanumeric (basic).
               Phase 2: code-aware — keeps np.array / read_csv / DataFrame
               intact alongside their parts, drops pure numbers. (Stemming
               was tried and dropped; see 02_results.txt.)
    k1 = 1.2   b = 0.75    (standard Lucene defaults)

DENSE RETRIEVAL (Phase 3)
    model      BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5 (768-d), fp16 on the GPU
    encode     documents plain; queries get bge's instruction prefix
    index      FAISS IndexFlatIP over L2-normalized vectors (= cosine, exact)
               HNSW/IVF is a drop-in swap when scaling to millions of docs
    truncation answers over ~512 tokens are cut (chunking deferred to Phase 7)

RAG

query → router (gemma, SEARCH-biased) 
        ├─ ANSWER → answer from model knowledge
        └─ SEARCH → retrieve top-4 (BM25+dense+LambdaMART)
                    └─ gate (top cosine ≥ 0.70?)
                        ├─ open  → answer grounded in docs
                        └─ closed → drop junk, answer alone
